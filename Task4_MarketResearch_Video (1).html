Risk Management

6 ESSENTIAL RISK STRATEGIES

1. DIVERSIFICATION
Never more than 5–10% in one asset. Spread across sectors, geographies & asset classes. Reduces unsystematic risk without cutting expected return.
2. POSITION SIZING
Never risk >2% of portfolio on a single trade. Crypto positions capped at 3–5% per coin. Larger positions require higher conviction and evidence.
3. STOP-LOSS ORDERS
15% trailing stop for stocks, 25% for crypto. Prevents emotional decisions during market panic. Protects capital for future opportunities.
4. DOLLAR-COST AVERAGING
Invest fixed amounts at regular intervals. Eliminates timing risk, reduces average cost in downturns. Ideal for volatile crypto positions.
5. HEDGING
Put options, inverse ETFs, gold/Treasury bonds as insurance. 5–10% bonds allocation reduces volatility by 15–20% without major return impact.
6. REBALANCING
Quarterly rebalance when any asset drifts ±5% from target. Auto-enforces buy low / sell high discipline. Maintains intended risk profile.
Behavioral Finance

THE BIGGEST RISK: YOURSELF

Behavioral biases destroy more wealth than market crashes

FOMO
Fear Of Missing Out drives buying at market tops. When everyone is excited, the price is already too high. Buy on fear, sell on greed.
LOSS AVERSION
Holding losing positions too long hoping for recovery. Losses feel 2x more painful than gains feel good. Cut losses early.
OVERCONFIDENCE
Leads to excessive concentration in favorite picks. Even professionals consistently underperform diversified index funds.
"TIME IN THE MARKET BEATS TIMING THE MARKET"
90%+ of investors underperform the market over the long run due to behavioral mistakes. Write a plan and stick to it.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

Stocks offer stability, crypto offers growth. A blended 70–80% stocks + 10–20% crypto portfolio maximizes risk-adjusted returns using low correlation between the two.

Risk management is non-negotiable. Diversification, stop-losses, DCA, and quarterly rebalancing are the foundation of every successful long-term portfolio.
