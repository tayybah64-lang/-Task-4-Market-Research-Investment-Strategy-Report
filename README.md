### 📊 Stock Market vs Cryptocurrency
- US Stock Market: $50T+ cap, Mon–Fri trading, 100+ year history, strong regulation
- Crypto Market: ~$3–4T cap, 24/7 trading, 15-year history, evolving regulation
- Low correlation (0.10–0.45) between the two = genuine diversification benefit

### 🛡️ Risk Management Strategies
1. **Diversification** — No single asset >5–10% of portfolio
2. **Position Sizing** — Never risk >2% on a single trade
3. **Stop-Loss Orders** — 15% for stocks, 25% for crypto
4. **Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)** — Fixed amounts at regular intervals
5. **Hedging** — Put options, inverse ETFs, bonds as insurance
6. **Rebalancing** — Quarterly review and rebalance back to targets
